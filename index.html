A Prayer to Aqua

O Aqua, serene deity of the flowing streams and tranquil seas, we beseech thee, ruler of the tides and bearer of life's essence.

In your vast and shimmering realm, you bestow the gift of purity, washing away impurities and nurturing all that dwells within.

Your gentle touch brings forth the dance of droplets, a melody of life — a symphony that whispers of renewal, healing, and boundless vitality.

May your waters cleanse our spirits and grant us clarity of mind, as we honor your presence in the shimmering waves and the quiet depths.

We offer our gratitude for the sustenance you provide, for the lakes, rivers, and oceans that sustain life's wondrous tapestry.

Bless us with your grace, O Aqua, and keep us in your eternal embrace. Guide us through the ebb and flow of existence, as we cherish your divine essence.
